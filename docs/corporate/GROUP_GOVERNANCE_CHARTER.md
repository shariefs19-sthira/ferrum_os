# Group Governance Charter

> **DRAFT — FOR REVIEW AND SETTLEMENT BY A PRACTICING CA, CS, AND LAWYER.** Not legal advice, an executed instrument, or a representation that any future entity exists. Names, structures, ownership and appointments remain **[OPERATOR-INPUT]** or **[OPERATOR-DECISION]**.

## Purpose

This charter separates each Ferrum product line and any legal entity by scope, authority, economics, records and intellectual property. It does not itself incorporate, appoint, allot shares, license IP or bind an entity.

## Governing doctrine

1. Sharief is sole Chief Founder and retains final authority over all non-execution matters, subject to mandatory law and formally adopted constitutional documents.
2. Shoaib is Head of Execution for Ferrum Projects/Builds only. His joint authority is confined to day-to-day physical execution within an approved project, budget and delegation. It creates no authority, ownership, veto, economic right or co-founder status in any other product line.
3. A co-founder, revenue interest, equity interest or management mandate is scope-exclusive to the registry entry and written instrument that grants it. Silence, assistance, referral, shared naming or group presentation creates none.
4. Every digital product, codebase, model, dataset, knowledge base, brand master, domain, specification and derivative remains Sharief Prior/Outside IP unless a signed instrument expressly states otherwise. No Day-1 execution entity acquires it.
5. Inter-entity services, outputs, licences, referrals, staff sharing, project allotments and cost allocations require separate written, arm's-length terms; applicable conflicts, valuation, tax and corporate approvals are verified professionally.
6. Each legal entity maintains separate accounts, bank authority, contracts, credentials, data stores, statutory records, insurance and liabilities. Divisions remain internal business scopes until an operator decision and lawful formation state otherwise.
7. No registry entry is evidence of incorporation, ownership, appointment or authority. The operative signed constitutional and contractual documents prevail.

## Reserved group matters

Sharief's prior written approval, plus every non-waivable corporate approval, is required for group structure, incorporation, ownership, securities, new co-founders, brand or IP use, cross-entity data access, financing, guarantees, related-party terms, mergers, disposals, product-line transfers and public claims about group membership.

## Admission and change control

A new entity or co-founder requires **[OPERATOR-DECISION]**, professional structure advice, an updated registry, settled constitutional documents, IP/data boundary schedules and arm's-length intercompany instruments. Changes are versioned; historic entries are not silently overwritten.

## Review gate

Before adoption, professionals must reconcile this charter with each entity's MoA/AoA, shareholders' and founders' agreements, employment terms, beneficial-ownership disclosures, competition restraints, tax position and applicable group-accounting requirements.
