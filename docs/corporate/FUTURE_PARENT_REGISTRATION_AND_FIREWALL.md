> **FUTURE DRAFT OUTLINE — NOT OPERATIVE ON DAY 1; FOR REVIEW AND SETTLEMENT BY A PRACTICING CA, CS, AND LAWYER.** Not legal, tax or filing advice. No parent is represented as incorporated. Every form, fee and timeline is **INDICATIVE-VERIFY-BEFORE-FILING**; every unknown is **[OPERATOR-INPUT]** or **[OPERATOR-DECISION]**.

## Activation gate

No step activates until the operator decides the parent purpose, legal form, ownership, capital, jurisdiction and relationship to Ferrum Projects: **[OPERATOR-DECISION]**. Professionals must compare holding-company, operating-company and personal-IP ownership consequences before name reservation or asset movement.

## Parent registration outline

1. Record commercial purpose, products in scope, excluded liabilities and target structure **[OPERATOR-INPUT]**.
2. Obtain CA/CS/lawyer advice on form, tax, beneficial ownership, foreign investment if any, related parties and whether a parent/subsidiary structure is warranted.
3. Clear name and trade-mark conflicts without assuming Ferrum brand ownership transfers.
4. Settle fresh MoA/AoA, capital, subscribers, directors, reserved matters, share rights, vesting and governance **[OPERATOR-DECISION]**.
5. Complete current official incorporation, office, tax, banking, labour and sector registrations only after same-day professional verification.
6. Adopt first-board controls, accounts, statutory registers, privacy/security roles and intercompany approval policy.
7. Execute each firewall instrument below before any asset, service, project, person, credential or data crosses an entity boundary.

## Non-transfer baseline

Formation of a parent, common ownership, shared director, consolidated marketing or intercompany payment does not itself move Ferrum OS IP, Ferrum Projects contracts, employees, liabilities, goodwill, data, licences or opportunities. Every transfer requires an identified asset/right, authority, consideration, tax/stamp analysis and signed instrument.
