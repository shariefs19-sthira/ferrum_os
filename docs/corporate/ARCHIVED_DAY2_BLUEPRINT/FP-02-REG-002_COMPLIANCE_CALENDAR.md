- GST registration/applicability, invoice controls, e-invoice/e-way bill, returns, payment, reconciliation and annual return: **[OPERATOR-INPUT: registration/profile]**; CA verifies current forms and cadence.
- TDS/TCS deduction, deposit, returns, certificates and reconciliations: sections, rates, challans and dates **INDICATIVE-VERIFY-BEFORE-FILING**.

- Preserve invoices, vouchers, bank reconciliations, project cost records and tax evidence under the controlled finance/cash and payment-annexure skeletons.

## Physical-construction compliance

For every project, counsel/safety adviser must create a jurisdiction-specific applicability sheet covering building permission/client responsibility, BOCW registration/cess, contract labour, interstate migrant workers, wages, EPF/ESI, safety plan, welfare, environmental consents, fire/electrical/lifting equipment, insurance, professional licences, material testing and local trade permissions. No item is assumed inapplicable merely because the client holds the principal permit.

Evidence retained: contract allocation, registration/licence, worker register, induction/toolbox records, inspection/test reports, incident records, wage/payment evidence, subcontractor compliance, cess evidence, insurance and closure/handover records.
