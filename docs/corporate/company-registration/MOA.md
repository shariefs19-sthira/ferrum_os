To undertake, execute, construct, erect, fabricate, assemble, repair, strengthen, renovate and complete reinforced-cement-concrete works and structural-steel works for buildings and civil structures; and to provide physical site mobilisation, construction supervision, site management, labour and subcontract coordination, material and plant deployment, measurement, quality control, safety coordination, testing, completion and handover services for those works.

## Incidental objects

The Company shall not pursue an activity outside the main object and the strictly incidental matters above unless its memorandum is first lawfully altered and all approvals are obtained.
