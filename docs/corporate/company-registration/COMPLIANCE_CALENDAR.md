# Simplified Compliance Calendar

> **[PROFESSIONAL-REVIEW-REQUIRED]** Control calendar only. Every form, fee, threshold and due date is **INDICATIVE-VERIFY-BEFORE-FILING** and must be maintained by the practicing CA/CS/lawyer.

Entity facts, incorporation date, financial year, state, capital, turnover, workforce, registrations and advisers: **[OPERATOR-INPUT]**.

## Incorporation and commencement

- Verify COI, CIN, PAN, TAN, filed MoA/AoA and statutory registers immediately after approval.
- Hold the first board meeting within 30 days of incorporation under section 173(1). Appoint the first auditor, open the bank account, receive subscription money, issue/share-stamp certificates and complete any applicable commencement filing within each current statutory period verified by the practicing CS.
- Complete registered-office, display, letterhead, GST, EPFO, ESIC, professional-tax, Shops/Establishment, BOCW, contract-labour and other applicable registrations on professional advice.

## Recurring company controls

| Cadence/trigger | Control |
|---|---|
| Every board cycle | Notice, agenda, conflicts, quorum, resolutions and minutes |
| Monthly | Bank/cash, receivables/payables, payroll, tax filings and project-cost reconciliation |
| Quarterly | Board dashboard, contracts, insurance, claims, safety, vendor/subcontractor compliance |
| Annual | Accounts, statutory audit, board report, AGM or lawful alternative, financial-statement filing and annual return |
| Director/auditor/capital event | Current approvals, registers, certificates, stamp duty and ROC filing |
| Related-party/borrowing/charge event | Disclosure, approvals, arm's-length support, registers and current filings |

## Project and workforce controls

Before each project, create an applicability sheet for contract responsibility, building permissions, BOCW/cess, labour, wages, EPF/ESI, safety, environmental/fire/electrical/lifting controls, insurance, testing and handover. Preserve contracts, approved drawings, worker records, inspections, test reports, incidents, measurements, invoices, payments and closure evidence.

## Annual professional register

For every applicable filing record statute/rule, live portal source/date, current form/version, due date, government fee/stamp duty, attachments, DSC/signatory, certification, SRN/challan and closure. Year/professional owners/exceptions: **[OPERATOR-INPUT]**.
