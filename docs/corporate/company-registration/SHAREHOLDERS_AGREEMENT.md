## Governance

- The proposed Board has three directors: Sharief nominates two and Shoaib nominates one, each subject to appointment under the Act and Articles; Sharief's nominees therefore form the Board majority.
- The chair has a casting vote where lawful and included in the finalized Articles.
- Shoaib has full operational participation within approved Execution Matters.

- Director duties and statutory shareholder rights cannot be contracted away.

## Companies Act and AoA supremacy

This agreement is subject to the Companies Act, 2013 and other applicable law. Under section 6, any provision repugnant to the Act is void to that extent. If this agreement conflicts with the Company's filed Articles of Association, the **AoA prevails** for Company governance and corporate action until lawfully amended. The shareholders must exercise lawful rights to align the documents, but no SHA term authorizes the Company, Board or member to act contrary to the Act or AoA.
