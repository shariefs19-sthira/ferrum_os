## Professional filing gate

The CA/CS must attach a dated verification sheet confirming live form names/numbers/versions; integrated DIN limits; eMoA/eAoA eligibility; AGILE-PRO-S registrations for the selected state; stamp duty; name-reservation validity; attachment recency; PAN/TAN route; INC-20A applicability; GST/EPFO/ESIC applicability; and every fee. A live-portal mismatch overrides this SOP.

## Official-source verification record

Official MCA materials accessed on 2026-09-09 confirmed that the V3 incorporation set includes SPICe+ Part A, SPICe+ Part B, INC-33, INC-34, INC-9 and AGILE-PRO-S, and that AGILE-PRO-S is the linked route for the stated integrated registrations. The official INC-20A instruction kit confirms professional certification of that webform. These materials do not establish that an older instruction kit, fee, state coverage or processing estimate remains current on filing day; therefore the live portal and current rules control.
