## Mandatory private-company restrictions

For section 2(68) compliance, the Articles shall:

1. restrict the right to transfer shares through the pre-emption, board-registration and permitted-transfer provisions settled by the practicing CS;
2. limit members to 200, excluding persons within statutory employee/former-employee exclusions and counting joint holders as one member as the Act requires; and
3. prohibit every invitation to the public to subscribe for any security of the Company.

The Company has exactly two directors: Sharief and Shoaib. No additional, alternate or nominee director may be appointed unless both shareholders unanimously amend these Articles and complete every mandatory approval and filing. ## Two-director Board and Permanent Chairman

1. Sharief and Shoaib are the only directors. Both must receive notice and, subject to section 174 and applicable exemptions, both constitute the Board quorum.
2. Sharief is Permanent Chairman while he remains a director. The Chairman calls meetings, approves a procedurally fair agenda, ensures papers circulate, and conducts discussion. He has **no tie-breaking vote**. Table F regulation 68(ii) is expressly excluded to the extent the practicing CS confirms that exclusion is lawful and registrable.
3. Neither domain authority nor this schedule authorizes one director to bypass a Board/member approval reserved by statute, conceal information, breach section 166 duties, act while conflicted, or represent that a deadlocked Board approved a matter.

## Schedule A — Reserved Matters and Decision Resolution

Sharief's domain comprises design, finance, strategy, non-site hiring and client contracting, subject to statute, approved budgets and the reserved matters. Shoaib's domain comprises site operations, contractor selection, project scheduling, safety compliance and site-staff hiring, within approved contracts, designs and budgets. Capital/share changes, borrowing, guarantees, disposals, dividends, related-party matters and constitutional amendments require joint consent and every approval required by law. A failed safety audit or imminent-danger finding automatically halts affected work until documented clearance.

**Matters requiring joint consent, domain authority, and objective resolution shall be governed by the Board-approved Threshold Schedule Annexure, as amended from time to time by ordinary Board Resolution.**

The Threshold Schedule Annexure cannot amend these Articles, reallocate a statutory Board/member power, relax directors' duties, or treat a 1–1 disagreement as approval. Any amendment must be validly passed with the required notice, quorum and majority. An unresolved matter remains unapproved unless an applicable domain rule or objective rule in the Annexure lawfully resolves it.
