Except where expressly modified below, the Company adopts the regulations in Schedule I, Table F applicable to a company limited by shares, as converted by the practicing CS into the current eAoA.

## Mandatory private-company restrictions

For section 2(68) compliance, the Articles shall:

1. restrict the right to transfer shares through the pre-emption, board-registration and permitted-transfer provisions settled by the practicing CS;
2. limit members to 200, excluding persons within statutory employee/former-employee exclusions and counting joint holders as one member as the Act requires; and
3. prohibit every invitation to the public to subscribe for any security of the Company.

The Company must have at least two subscribers/members and at least two directors at incorporation and thereafter, subject to the Act. To make the selected majority-director package operational, the proposed initial Board has three directors: Sharief plus one additional Sharief nominee, and one Shoaib nominee (who may be Shoaib), each validly appointed. ## Board-control package

1. Sharief may nominate two directors on a three-director Board and Shoaib may nominate one, only through appointment mechanisms validly written into the Articles and completed under sections 149, 152 and, where relevant, 161. A nomination is not itself an appointment. Any Board-size change must preserve the settled control design through lawful Articles/SHA amendments and appointments.
2. Sharief chairs board and member meetings only after valid appointment/election. The chair has a casting vote at a board meeting only where Table F/final Articles and law permit; no casting vote applies at a member meeting unless lawfully provided, overrides a mandatory threshold, cures lack of quorum, or displaces a director's duty.
3. Shoaib participates in decisions within Execution Matters: day-to-day RCC and steel site delivery, approved sequencing, labour, plant, materials, subcontract coordination, measurement, quality, safety and handover within approved contracts and budgets.
4. Sharief's written approval, plus every mandatory corporate approval, is required for Non-Execution reserved matters: strategy; new services; tenders and contracts outside approved limits; capital or share changes; borrowing, guarantees or security; bank mandates; dividends; appointment/removal of directors or senior staff; related-party transactions; material litigation; acquisitions, disposals or restructuring; annual budgets; and commitments outside approved project scope.
5. An execution disagreement is decided by Sharief after Shoaib's written execution-risk note is considered. This does not displace statutory duties.
