# Online Incorporation Platform Research — India

> **[PROFESSIONAL-REVIEW-REQUIRED]** Procurement research only; not legal, tax or filing advice. Prices, inclusions and timelines are a public-web snapshot dated **2026-09-09** and must be reconfirmed in a written quotation before purchase. Government fees and stamp duty vary by capital, State and filing facts.

## Decision summary

Do **not** buy a low-cost incorporation package until the provider gives written confirmation that its practicing CS will transpose the operator-approved custom MoA and AoA into the MCA V3 eMoA/eAoA, reconcile the separate Founders' Agreement and SHA, and return a redline without substituting boilerplate. None of the five reviewed public product pages publishes a customer-controlled “upload custom MoA/AoA/SHA” commitment.

The lowest-risk procurement route for this pack is therefore: procure two individual Class-3 DSCs from a licensed Certifying Authority or transparently priced reseller; obtain DINs through SPICe+ for the proposed first directors; and retain an independent practicing CS on a fixed-fee, written scope covering document settlement, professional certification, MCA submission, resubmissions and post-incorporation deliverables. Do not treat a marketplace's “MoA/AoA drafting” line item as acceptance of custom instruments.

## Critical custom-document finding

Searches of the providers' public product pages and indexed FAQ/terms material for “custom AoA”, “custom AOA upload”, “custom MoA”, “custom SHA” and equivalent wording found **no affirmative published workflow** for any reviewed provider. This is an evidence gap, not proof that a provider refuses bespoke documents. The procurement gate is a written answer from the actual certifying professional—not a sales assurance—that confirms:

1. the supplied physical-execution-only object clause will be used as the drafting instruction;
2. the two-director, no-casting-vote constitutional framework and Threshold Schedule Annexure reference will be reconciled into the filed AoA;
3. the Founders' Agreement and SHA will be reviewed for consistency but will remain separate agreements rather than misrepresented as MCA attachments;
4. every change to supplied text will be returned as a redline for founder approval before signing; and
5. professional certification, first resubmission and correction of provider-caused defects are included in a fixed fee.

## Recommended procurement sequence

1. Ask Vakilsearch, IndiaFilings and Corpbiz for the same written custom-document scope. Treat silence, “our standard format only”, or a refusal to redline as a rejection.
2. If one provides a named practicing CS, conflict-free engagement letter, fixed all-in statutory-cost schedule and the five confirmations above, select on the written quote—not the banner price.
3. If none does, purchase two MCA-compatible Class-3 DSCs independently. The provider's public ranges put an individual DSC broadly around **₹1,000–₹2,000 per director**, but obtain a licensed-provider quote including token, video verification, shipping and term.
4. Have the independent CS apply for the proposed first directors' DINs within SPICe+ where eligible, settle eMoA/eAoA fields, certify the incorporation set, file on MCA V3 and handle the agreed resubmission.
5. Keep the repo drafts out of platform portals until counsel issues execution copies; never upload KYC scans or credentials to this repository.

## Hidden-cost checklist for quotations

- MCA name-reservation and incorporation fees, authorised-capital slab and PAN/TAN charges;
- State-specific MoA/AoA stamp duty and any registered-office instrument duty;
- GST on professional services;
- two DSC certificates, physical tokens, video verification, delivery and renewal term;
- notarisation/apostille/certified translation where a subscriber or document requires it;
- name-reservation retries, validity-window expiry, ROC resubmissions and provider-error corrections;
- bespoke MoA/AoA redlining, SHA/Founders' Agreement consistency review and first-board pack;
- post-incorporation INC-20A, auditor appointment, share certificates/stamping and annual-compliance upsells.
